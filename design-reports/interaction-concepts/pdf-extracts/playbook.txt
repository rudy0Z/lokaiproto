Conversational AI
UX &Product Design
Conversational AI shifts how people interact with products—from rigid visual controls like
buttons, forms, and nested menus to intent-driven natural language, context-aware
dialogue, and dynamic multimodal interfaces.
In this shift, designers architect natural interaction logic, context retention, failure recovery, and human-in-the-loop
safeguards. Our craft moves beyond drawing static, deterministic wireframes to designing adaptive experiences that
understand people, meet them in context, and gracefully handle the unexpected.

Why This Is the
Most Demanding
Design Skill in 2027
01
Shift to Intent-Based
& Agentic Software
Software is transitioning from static
clickpaths to autonomous agents executing
multi-step workflows. Designers must map
system behavior, logic, and operational
boundaries rather than fixed screens.
Bridging the AI Trust Gap
Product drop-off in AI tools rarely stems
from underlying model capability. It stems
from UX failures such as ambiguous
system feedback, lost context, or lack of
control. Transparent feedback loops are
critical to building user trust.
Multimodal Orchestration
Products increasingly combine voice, text,
computer vision, and real-time generated
visual widgets. Seamless transitions between
speaking, typing, reading, and tapping
require specialized conversational expertries.
DESIGN
IMPLICATION
Map behavior, not just screens.
Make system feedback legible and timely.
Preserve user control at every step.
Design seamless transitions between modalities.

Six steps to design conversations that work in the real world.
Start with intent and entities.
Preserve context across multi-turn dialogue.
Define branch points, clarifying questions,
boundaries, and fallback behavior.

Add transparent feedback, authorization
gates, and instant undo for risky actions.
