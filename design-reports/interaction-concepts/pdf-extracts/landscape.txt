2. MACHINE LEARNING AND PREDICTIVE AI
Machine Learning and
Predictive AI
From learning patterns to predicting what's next.
Machine Learning (ML)
ML enables systems to learn patterns from data
rather than relying only on explicit rules.
Leaves from historical data.
Finds patterns and relationships.
Improves with more data and experience.
Predictive AI
Predictive AI uses historical data and learned patterns to
forecast and support better decisions.
Forecast outcomes (e.g., demand, sales, risk).
Classify situations (e.g., customer segments).
Detect anomalies (e.g., fraud, system issues).
Support decisions with data-driven insights.
MACHINE LEARNING
Leads patterns.
Leaves from data to discover
patterns and relationships
without being explicitly
programmed.
VS
PREDICTIVE AI
Uses patterns to estimate
what may happen next.
Applies learned patterns to
forecast, classify, detect, and
support better decisions.
