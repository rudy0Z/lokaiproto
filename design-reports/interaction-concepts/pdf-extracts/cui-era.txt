1.
What is
Conversational UI Design?
Conversational UI Design is the practice of designing interactions where users communicate with a product through natural language—using
text, voice, or multimodal input.
The product understands the user, responds, asks questions, takes
actions, guides tasks, and provides feedback.
Conversational UI is not simply a chat box with AI.
It is about creating meaningful, task-oriented conversations
that help users achieve their goals efficiently.
Traditional UI: Step-by-step navigation
Conversational UI: Intent-driven interaction
