Refund for a damaged mixer

MUMBAI SUBURBAN, MH · OPENED 8 JUNE

“I bought a mixer grinder for ₹8,999. It arrived damaged and the seller refused a refund. What can I do?”

PRIYA · TODAY 11:14

When a product arrives damaged, that can count as a consumer issue. You can ask the seller for repair, replacement, or a refund. If they ignore a fair written ask, you can follow up with a legal notice.

In your case — Your mixer arrived damaged and the seller refused a replacement. The invoice, damage photos, and written refusal give you a solid next step.

4/5SOURCED

Consumer Protection Act, 2019

S.2(10) — defect in goods
S.35 — complaint before commission

Example — verify with a lawyer

  1. 1

    Send a written demand notice by registered post with acknowledgement due (AD), and keep the postal receipt.

  2. 2

    Keep the invoice, seller-chat screenshots, order ID, and defect photos together.

  3. 3

    If the seller ignores it, file on e-Daakhil or call National Consumer Helpline (1915) — the District Consumer Commission hears the complaint.
