## The two-minute read

**LokAI is a working, deterministic AI legal-guide prototype with enforcement-gated design
governance** — built to answer the three questions a 2026 design hiring manager actually asks:

1. *Can this person build systems, not just screens?*
2. *Can they design AI that people trust?*
3. *Does the work survive contact with a real phone?*

## Standout evidence — lead with these

1. **Design law as executable code.** A token architecture "named by job, never by hue"
   (`T.ground`, `T.anchor`, `T.flag`…), enforced by repo scripts (`verify:demo`, `ui-debt`
   ratchet) that fail the build on violations — contrast gates parse both themes; raw styles
   are structurally impossible outside the system files.
2. **AI trust surfaces built to the 2026 bar.** Visible grounding (grounded/total claims —
   never fake percentage confidence), tiered stop states that visibly refuse unsafe next steps,
   human-review gates before anything leaves the app. This is the transparency + HITL +
   escalation pattern that 2026 sources call the differentiator (Smashing Magazine, agentic
   design guides), not the table-stakes minimum.
3. **Legal-AI grounding discipline** in exactly the field where hallucinated citations are a
   named public risk (factor.law, wiley.law) — sources are inspectable, claims are scoped.
4. **Four-script typography** (EN/HI/MR/TA) with script-aware line-heights, variable-font
   tuning, and a documented anti-generic font rationale — an India-market alignment the
   government's own UX guidelines call essential (uxdt.nic.in).
5. **Shipped and traceable.** Every design decision has a doc, a token, and a gate. 2026
   hiring signals reward shipped, traceable work over polished case-study mocks.

## Honest gaps — name them before the reviewer does

The app was audited against a 35-criteria skills library + the 2026 market (13 screens, mobile
and desktop separately). Result: governance core passes nearly everywhere; the gaps are the
"last 5%" of feel: press physics, mobile-native hardening (safe areas, 16px inputs), desktop
dark-mode wiring, OKLCH color pipeline. All have dated, one-line-to-one-afternoon fixes queued
in the audit roadmap — closeable in ~1 focused day, each provable via the repo's own gates.

> The line to say out loud: *"I audited my own app against a 35-criteria skills library and the
> 2026 market, mobile and desktop separately. Here's the gap list and the fix queue."*
> That sentence is itself the differentiator — judgment, honesty, and systems thinking in one.
