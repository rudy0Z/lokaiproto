01 · Executive summary

Import sparingly — LokAI's system is the asset; borrow quality, not identity

The headline: 6 of 18 sources offer components that would visibly raise LokAI's ceiling; 4 more are useful niche picks; 8 are redundant with what LokAI already builds better in-house — or are the wrong medium (prompt packs, flash collections). The single most valuable discovery is that the highest-quality sources (BoardUI, AI Elements, BeautifulUI, transitions.dev) are all MIT/Apache, all token-driven, and all speak the exact interaction language LokAI's audit said it lacks: thinking states, HITL approval cards, reasoning streams, citation primitives, and press physics.

Tier 1 — adopt & adapt

AI Elements (inline-citation, sources, confirmation, reasoning) · BoardUI (agent-thinking, agent-log, segmented-control) · transitions.dev (thinking-states, streaming-text, spinner-to-check) · interior.dev (press micro-interactions — the C-10 fix)

Tier 2 — targeted picks

orb-ui (voice orb for the prepared-voice screen) · BeautifulUI (HITL approval primitives) · Halaska UI (38 AI-UX patterns as reference anatomy) · RuneIcons (conditional icon migration — see §6)

Tier 3 — pass / reference only

Amicro (redundant with motion tokens) · cube-motion (second motion engine = risk) · Spectrum/Spell/Rare/Componentry/ReactBits (quality varies; a few gems inside) · VibePrompt (prompts, not components) · AICSS (styling shortcut that bypasses LokAI's token law) · FluidFunctionalism (identity-forward, not system-forward)

Premium-white verdict preview (full study §7): white raises text contrast (17.21→18.25:1) and modernizes the look, but it breaks LokAI's differentiation — the warm-paper world is the "physical desk" metaphor recruiters remember, and pure white is what every AI UI kit already ships by default. Recommendation: keep cream as brand ground; adopt premium white as a new "focus mode" surface (Stop states, Draft paper) where its clinical calm serves comprehension. Rollback = zero (nothing changes by default).

06 · Icon decision

RuneIcons — conditional go, not a swap

The evidence

RuneIcons (runeicons.com, by Nexvyn): 900+ glyphs × 5 styles (outline, duotone, fill, pixelated, glass), browser path editor → SVG/JSX, tree-shakeable React/Vue, Apache-2.0. Health: 521★, pushed 2026-09-12. Risk: no npm package found under runeicons / rune-icons / @nexvyn/runeicons — distribution is repo-copy or unindexed.

LokAI current: three-tier doctrine (AGENTS.md, enforced): ① bespoke SVG glyphs (composer send/attach/mic in ai/Turn.tsx), ② @animateicons/react aliased set for chrome/gestures (ui/icons.tsx), ③ @phosphor-icons/react weight-controlled static icons in reading surfaces — 6 files, ~30 imports.

Verdict: NO-GO for swap · GO for 2-3 hero glyphs

A full swap costs 6-file migration and adds a fourth icon tier — violating the do-not-mix doctrine — for a marginal style gain over Phosphor's weights. And without an npm package, dependency hygiene suffers (supply-chain review burden, no version pinning).

Conditional use: RuneIcons' duotone style for 2-3 hero moments that deserve extra richness — the verification-gate shield, the evidence-locker mark, the stop-state glyph. Copy SVG into the bespoke tier (tier ①), recolor through T.attest/T.flag tokens. That's the sanctioned slot for hand-placed glyphs.

Revisit if: an npm package ships, or a LokAI v2 redesign re-opens the icon system.

Sources for the taste axis: bscwebdesign.at (off-whites read warmer/more modern), supercharge.design (pure white safe with non-pure-black text), ux.stackexchange.com (white cards float on neutral grounds), designmodo (crispness camp). Full links in §10.

7.2 What the renders show

Cream (current): the warm ground makes white cards genuinely float — the contact-shadow doctrine has physical logic; washes (blue/gold/rose) sit in the same temperature family; the whole surface reads as a paper desk. This is the identity the audit graded as "the strongest suit" (C-34 PASS both platforms).

Premium white: clinically crisp, slightly more modern to Northern-European eyes, and contrast numbers improve ~6%. But: cards lose the built-in lift (must lean harder on shadows — visible in the renders as heavier shadow stacks), warm washes need a full re-tune to avoid a yellow cast, and the result looks like every Linear/Intercom/Claude-grade AI product — i.e., it removes the differentiation that makes LokAI memorable in a portfolio. The skills library itself would score the swap as a taste regression (C-34: "coherent visual language" is already earning top marks).

Recommendation — KEEP CREAM as the brand ground. The recruiter story ("why is this warm when every AI app is white?") is stronger than the 6% contrast gain. But adopt premium white (#FFFFFF ground + #FAFBFC raised + desaturated washes) as a new purpose surface, not a theme switch: ① the Stop states (tonal opposite doctrine already calls for it), ② the Draft document paper (a "clean sheet of paper" reading mode), ③ optionally the print/export stylesheet. Where clinical calm serves comprehension, white wins; where brand memory is made, cream wins.
Rollback condition (as required): if any white surface experiment ships and users/readers describe LokAI as "generic" or "cold" in feedback, revert the surface tokens — a single --role-* re-point in index.css restores cream everywhere (zero call-site edits, by design of the token system). Nothing in the current app changes under this recommendation.
