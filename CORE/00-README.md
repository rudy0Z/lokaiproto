## The product in one paragraph

LokAI is a deterministic, zero-network prototype of an Indian legal-AI guide. Priya bought a
damaged ₹8,999 mixer grinder; the seller refused a replacement; she has no lawyer, no legal
vocabulary, a 390px phone, possibly Hindi/Marathi/Tamil, and about ten minutes of attention.
LokAI organises her facts, explains the law in plain registers, prepares a demand notice she
reviews line by line, and — the thesis — **completes what it can defend, names the exact edge
of what it will not guess, and hands her the rest with the work intact.** Every response is a
typed decision with a receipt. No live AI, no network, no fabricated confidence — by enforced
build gate, not by promise.

## The one rule that outranks the rest

**Receipts are computed, never authored.** Authored trust signals are trust theater — the single
failure this product cannot survive. Every design decision in this folder serves that sentence.
