# Direct

## Style profile

Default product profile with preserved LokAI editorial identity. This is a refinement, not a rebrand.

## Feel

Calm, grounded, and serious without feeling institutional. The interface should feel like a patient legal-aid guide: one decision at a time, plain language first, evidence available when requested.

## Palette

- Warm neutral canvas and white working surfaces.
- Deep navy for primary action, active navigation, and trustworthy structure.
- Saffron for limited emphasis and progress, never as the default edge on every card.
- Oxblood only for stop/high-risk states.
- Green only for confirmed facts and directly supported claims.
- All normal text combinations meet WCAG 2.2 AA.

## Type

- Fraunces: page and matter titles only.
- Inter: interface labels, controls, body copy, legal explanations.
- JetBrains Mono: source identifiers and evidence metadata.
- Noto Sans families: content in Indian scripts.
- No display serif in buttons, compact labels, or data.

## Layout concept

- Mobile: focused single-column journey with persistent bottom navigation and one primary action near the thumb zone.
- Tablet: two-column content with navigation rail.
- Desktop: left navigation, central matter workspace, right contextual panel for sources and legal-term explanations.
- Guardrail states change available actions and content hierarchy, not the entire chrome.

## Signature

The source-backed answer stack: a plain-language three-part response connected to inspectable facts, official sources, and a visible review boundary. Legal terms are discreetly highlighted and reveal simple explanations without leaving the task.

## Rejected defaults

- Generic chatbot bubbles as the full product.
- Marketing hero as the desktop workspace.
- Confidence percentages without a derivation.
- Repeated colored side stripes for every status.
- Nested card grids, decorative gradients, glass panels, and dead controls.
- Side navigation on compact phones.

## Gate

PASS — The direction preserves the recognizable identity, names a task-specific signature, and rejects the existing patterns that weaken trust and coherence.
