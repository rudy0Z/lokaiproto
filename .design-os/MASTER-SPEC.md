# Bridge

## Run context

T2 existing React/Vite redesign, default style, agentic-AI pack, regulated legal-content risk. This is a deterministic portfolio prototype; all legal content is illustrative and awaits qualified review.

## Product contract

Demonstrate how LokAI helps a first-time user understand and prepare a response to one consumer-refund matter while visibly stopping when facts, sources, scope, or risk make self-service unsafe. No live APIs, permissions, authentication, payments, production storage, or lawyer access.

## Experience contract

The same matter and scenario state appears across desktop and mobile. Users can inspect supporting facts and sources, correct facts, understand legal terms, review drafts, and observe explicit guardrail states. Compact phones use bottom navigation; tablets use a rail; desktop uses sidebar plus contextual right panel.

## Visual contract

Preserve the warm editorial LokAI identity. Use Fraunces for titles, Inter for UI/body, JetBrains Mono for evidence, and Noto for Indian scripts. Navy structures the workspace; saffron is limited emphasis; green and oxblood are semantic. Use flat, readable surfaces with AA contrast and restrained motion.

## Content contract

Lead with plain language, define legal terms in context, state missing information directly, and never imply legal certainty. Replace technical AI labels with observable evidence language. Every source is labelled `Illustrative · expert review pending` until qualified review.

## Interaction contract

Prepared samples drive a typed scenario engine through four short announced progress stages. Policy tiers control actions: self-service may explain and prepare; review-recommended must collect or disclose; human-help-required stops and preserves a summary. Legal terms open a mobile dialog or populate the desktop right panel.

## Build sequence

1. Product/design docs and typed shared data.
2. Demo context and scenario engine.
3. Legal-term and guardrail components.
4. Shared chat presenter and deterministic inputs.
5. Desktop/mobile navigation and matter continuity.
6. Remove live capability and commercial claims.
7. Token, accessibility, and responsive cleanup.
8. Build, zero-network checks, route checks, screenshots, final audit.
